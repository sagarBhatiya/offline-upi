How Offline UPI Works

Designed to emulate the frictionless convenience of UPI while removing the requirement for internet connectivity during transfer.

1

Hotspot Pairing (QR 1)

Merchant turns on Personal Hotspot. Screen shows Hotspot QR 1 (WIFI:S:...). Payer scans with in-app camera to connect to the local Wi-Fi subnet automatically without entering passwords.

Result: Zero-latency peer socket ready at 192.168.43.1
2

Offline Payment (QR 2)

Merchant locks amount and displays Dynamic Payment QR 2. Payer scans with camera, confirms, and money is debited from Payer and credited to Merchant offline with a live voice announcement!

Result: Instant balance transfer + Monotonic Nonce audit
3

Bank Withdrawal

Merchant stores offline earnings in their local vault. Whenever the device reconnects to internet, the merchant initiates a 1-tap withdrawal to their Bank Account + IFSC or Real Bank UPI ID via IMPS.

Result: Direct settlement to real bank account with Bank UTR

Cryptographic Safeguards

Bank-Grade On-Device Security

Hardware KeyStore SECP256R1 Signatures

Every offline voucher payload is signed with the user's private key stored inside the Android KeyStore Hardware Security Module (HSM). The merchant validates the signature against the payer's public key offline.

Monotonic Sequence Anti-Replay Guard

Each voucher carries an atomic monotonically increasing counter. If a malicious client tries to retransmit a previously used voucher, the receiver immediately quarantines the payload and rejects the payment.

₹500 Offline Transaction Cap

Strictly enforces RBI/NPCI guidelines for offline micro-payments. Single offline transactions are limited to ₹500 to minimize exposure while allowing everyday essential commerce.

ACID Double-Entry Accounting Invariant

Both sender and receiver state changes are recorded as complementary debit and credit entries in SQLite. Mathematical audit guarantees Sum(DR) == Sum(CR) across all offline transactions.

Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Do devices need active cellular data or SIM balance? ▼

No! Both devices connect strictly over a local Wi-Fi Hotspot created by the receiver. Zero cellular data, SIM network, or internet is required during payment.

What prevents a user from spending the same offline money twice? ▼

Every transaction voucher contains an atomic monotonic sequence nonce signed cryptographically with ECDSA SECP256R1. The receiver verifies that the nonce is strictly greater than the sender's last known nonce; duplicate or replayed nonces are instantly quarantined.

How does the merchant convert offline money into real bank funds? ▼

Whenever the merchant reconnects to internet (at home or via Wi-Fi), they go to the "Bank Withdrawal" section in the app and tap "Withdraw to Bank". Funds are disbursed via IMPS to their Bank Account or Bank UPI ID.

What is the maximum limit for offline payments? ▼

In compliance with offline payment frameworks, individual transactions are capped at ₹500 to minimize risk.
